Pose and idle

Arms stay behind the outfit and rotate at the shoulder; the head turns at the base of the neck. Idle adds slow breathing and a slight sway, plus a small nod while he talks.

Eyes

Random blinks every 2.5 to 5.5 seconds, with an occasional double blink.
